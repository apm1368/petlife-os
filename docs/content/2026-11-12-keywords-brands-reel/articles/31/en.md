We used four plain criteria:

- **Long history:** every company has been making pet food or pet products for decades.

## Dry food brands

### Royal Canin

Royal Canin is a French brand founded in 1968 by Jean Cathary, a veterinary surgeon in the south of France. Its head office is in Aimargues, and it has been part of Mars, Incorporated since the early 2000s. Royal Canin is known above all for formulas designed around breed, body size and life stage, and for a range of veterinary diets that are used on a vet's recommendation.

### Hill's Pet Nutrition

Hill's is an American company that has been owned by Colgate-Palmolive since 1976. Its pet food story goes back to 1948, when Dr Mark Morris Sr., a veterinarian, contracted with the Hill Packing Company in Topeka, Kansas, to produce a food he had developed. Hill's Science Diet, created in 1968 and sold as Science Plan in the UK and Europe, covers everyday food by life stage; Prescription Diet is its range of veterinary diets, used on a vet's advice.

### Purina Pro Plan

Pro Plan belongs to Nestlé Purina PetCare, the St. Louis-based pet food arm of the Swiss group Nestlé, formed in 2001 when Nestlé acquired Ralston Purina and merged it with its own pet food business. The brand itself is older: Purina introduced it in 1986. Pro Plan covers dogs and cats of different ages and sizes, and in many countries a Pro Plan Veterinary Diets range is sold for use under veterinary supervision.

### Josera

Josera is a German family business based in Kleinheubach, Bavaria. Its roots go back to 1941, when Joseph and Pauline Erbacher set up a plant producing feed lime for livestock. The company moved to Kleinheubach in 1980 and started making pet food in 1988; according to Josera, its products are now sold in more than 80 countries.

### Farmina

Farmina is an Italian family company. Its story begins in 1965, when Francesco Russo founded an animal nutrition business called Russo Mangimi. In 1999 his son, Dr Angelo Russo, took the company into pet food, and it is still run by the Russo family. Its flagship line is N&D, short for Natural & Delicious, which includes dry and wet recipes for dogs and cats.

### Brit

Brit is a Czech brand made by VAFO Praha, a privately owned family company founded in 1994 in Chrášťany, near Prague. VAFO Praha later became the core of the larger VAFO Group, whose other brands include Carnilove. Brit's main lines are Brit Premium by Nature, aimed at everyday feeding, and Brit Care, which the company presents as its super-premium range.

## Pet gear brands

With gear, size, material and build quality matter more than the logo. To compare options side by side, open the [dog and cat accessories category in the opetlife shop](/en/shop/products?category=accessories).

### Trixie

Trixie is a German company founded in 1974 and headquartered in Tarp, in northern Germany. Founder Bonnik Hansen and his wife started with a small shop; today, according to the company, Trixie offers about 6,000 items for dogs, cats, birds, small animals and reptiles, from toys and beds to leads and scratching posts.

### Ferplast

Ferplast is an Italian family company founded in 1966 by Carlo Vaccari and his sister Caterina in the province of Vicenza. Its first products were bird cages, and its headquarters are now in Castelgomberto. It is known mainly for carriers, cages, dog and cat beds, litter trays and covered litter boxes, and aquariums. If you're choosing a tray, our guide to [types of cat litter](/en/blog/cat-litter-types) helps you pick what goes in it.

### KONG

KONG is an American company founded in 1976 by Joe Markham in Golden, Colorado, where it is still based. Its signature product is the hollow, snowman-shaped rubber toy you can fill with food or treats, and even freeze, to keep a dog busy for longer. Choose the size by your dog's build and chewing strength.

### flexi

flexi is a German family business known for retractable leads. Manfred Bogdahn came up with the idea in 1972 while walking his dog in Hamburg, and the company started in 1973. It is based in Bargteheide, near Hamburg, where it says it designs and produces its leads. Each model is rated for a maximum dog weight, so buy by weight.

## Best pet food brands: start with your pet, not the logo

Each brand sells dozens of products for very different animals. Before you compare names, be clear about:

- **Life stage:** kitten or puppy, adult or senior.
- **Size:** for dogs, from toy breeds to giant breeds.
- **Neutering and activity:** neutered or less active pets usually need fewer calories.
- **"Complete" food:** a food labelled "complementary" is not meant to be the whole daily diet.
- **Portions:** adjust to your pet's weight and body condition, not only the table on the bag.
- **Medical needs:** if your pet has a condition or food sensitivity, the choice belongs with your vet.

We go deeper in our guides to the [best dry cat food](/en/blog/best-dry-cat-food) and the [best dry dog food](/en/blog/best-dry-dog-food). The World Small Animal Veterinary Association ([WSAVA](https://wsava.org/)) also suggests asking manufacturers, rather than trusting marketing: do they employ a qualified nutritionist, who formulates the food, who actually makes it and how is quality controlled? If you switch brands, mix the new food into the old one gradually over 7 to 10 days.

## How to tell a genuine product from a fake

Counterfeit, expired or repackaged pet food does turn up, especially from unknown marketplace sellers. Check every purchase:

1. Buy from the brand's authorised retailers, a well-known pet shop or your vet. For online orders, read our [guide to buying from an online pet shop](/en/blog/online-pet-shop-guide).
2. Check that the bag is factory-sealed, with intact seams, no tape or re-stitching and no holes.
3. Find the batch number and best-before date printed on the bag itself. If a sticker covers them or they look erased, don't buy it.
4. Make sure the label names the company responsible and gives an address.
5. Compare the packaging with photos on the brand's official website: colours, design, weight and languages.
6. After opening, if the kibble smells rancid or shows mould or insects, don't feed it and contact the seller.
7. For gear, look for the brand name on the product itself, instructions and a size or maximum-weight label.

[[INFOGRAPHIC]]

At home, keep dry food in its original bag inside a closed container, somewhere cool and dry.

:::warning
The most costly mistakes: buying loose kibble from an open sack with no label, buying a bag whose best-before date is covered or erased, and swapping a veterinary diet for another brand without asking your vet. If your pet has repeated vomiting or diarrhoea or refuses to eat after starting a new food, stop it and call your vet.
:::

## FAQ about pet food and pet gear brands

:::faq
? What are the best pet food brands?
No brand is best for every animal. The right food depends on your pet's age, size, neutering status, activity and health, so choose a specific product for your pet's needs, ideally with your vet.
? Can I buy veterinary diets without a vet?
It's better not to. Veterinary diets are designed for animals a vet has examined, so leave starting, stopping or switching them to your vet.
? How can I tell if pet food is genuine?
A genuine bag is factory-sealed, has the batch number and best-before date printed on it and names the responsible company. Avoid resealed bags, covered dates and loose food sold by weight.
