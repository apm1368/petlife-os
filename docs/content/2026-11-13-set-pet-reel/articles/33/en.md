**Set Pet** is opetlife's made-to-order service for **matching outfits for you and your pet**: a T-shirt, hoodie or sweatshirt for you and an outfit for your pet, printed with your own photos or designs. You choose the garment, colour and size, upload your photos or designs, place them on the chest, back or sleeves, and pick your pet's size with the size guide. Before anything is printed you get a print proof, and you pay once you approve it.
:::

A photo of you and your dog in the same design is the kind of picture you keep for years. Set Pet makes that easy: you design one print, and it goes on your garment and on the back of your pet's outfit, each in its own size. The 90-second video below shows the whole process.

## How Set Pet works, step by step

1. **Garment, colour and size:** choose a T-shirt, hoodie or sweatshirt in one of six colours (white, black, grey, navy, cream or green), then your size from S to 3XL. Your height and weight give a size suggestion.
2. **Photos and designs:** upload photos or designs of you and your pet, for example your pet's face, a photo of the two of you and a small drawing. There is no fixed number, and you can also add a short text such as your pet's name.
3. **Place them on the T-shirt:** put each image on the front, the back or an upper sleeve, then switch between the front and back views to check both sides. One design can go on more than one place.
4. **Your pet's outfit and size:** choose a T-shirt or vest, a hoodie or a bandana for your pet, pick its size with the size guide, and your design goes on the back of the outfit. Its colour matches yours unless you change it.
5. **Approve the print proof:** place your order. Our team prepares a print proof, a picture of exactly what will be printed, and you pay once you approve it. Set Pet is made to order, not an off-the-shelf shop item.

## How to find your pet's size

Pet outfits come in sizes S to 3XL, and the size guide shows the measurements for each size. To find the right one, use a soft tape measure while your pet stands calmly. A treat helps.

- **Neck:** around the base of the neck, where a collar sits. Leave room for two fingers between the tape and the neck.
- **Chest:** around the widest part of the rib cage, just behind the front legs. This is the measurement that matters most.
- **Back length:** along the back from the base of the neck to the base of the tail.

Compare your numbers with the size guide and choose by the chest. If your pet falls between two sizes, check the back length too, and for a very deep chest or a thick coat, choose the larger size.

## Photos and designs that print well

- Use sharp, well-lit photos in which your pet's face is clearly visible. The original photo from your phone camera is better than a screenshot or a photo sent through a messenger app. The builder warns you if a photo is too small to print sharply.
- Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC) and print files (PDF, SVG, AI or PSD) up to 50 MB are accepted.
- Strong, simple shapes work best on small areas such as the sleeves.
- Only upload photos and designs that you own or have permission to use. Logos and characters that belong to other brands can't be printed.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
? Is Set Pet a ready-made product in the shop?
No. Every Set Pet is made to order with your own design and sizes. Before printing you get a print proof, and nothing is printed until you approve it.
? How is the price worked out?
From a fixed price list: each garment and each print area has its price. You see a price range while you build your set and the exact price when you review your order.
? When do I pay?
After you approve the print proof. You can still change your order until then.
? Can I cancel my order?
Yes, free of charge until printing starts. After that, a refund is only possible if there is a printing defect.
? Can I order just for myself?
A set is meant for you and your pet together. If your pet has passed away, you can order your own garment alone, for example with their photo.
? What if my design can't be printed?
If a design isn't suitable or belongs to someone else, our team tells you why, and you can send another one.
:::
